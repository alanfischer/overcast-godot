# Star catalogue

`stars.res` is the Yale Bright Star Catalogue, 5th revised edition (Hoffleit & Warren, 1991; CDS
V/50), as the Smithsonian Astrophysical Observatory distributes it
(http://tdc-www.harvard.edu/catalogs/bsc5.html): positions (J2000), V magnitudes and B−V colours
of its 9,110 stars, baked by `tools/bake_stars.gd`.
